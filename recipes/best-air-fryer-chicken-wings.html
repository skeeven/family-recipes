Best Air Fryer Chicken Wings

Air FryerChicken
Servings6
PrepPT5M
CookPT20M

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Chicken wings (drums and flats) (defrosted if frozen)
  • 2 tsp Herbs de Provence ((or Italian Seasoning))
  • 1 1/2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp Onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp Poultry seasoning (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp Sea Salt (or to taste)
  • 1/4 tsp Black Pepper (or to taste)
  • 2 tsp Baking powder, optional (aluminum-free)
  • Cooking Spray

Directions

  1. Clean and pat dry your chicken wings with a paper towel. Add the chicken to a large bowl with the seasonings and baking powder. Make sure all the wings are coated in the seasoning.
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 380°F for 3 minutes.
  3. Lightly spray the air fryer basket with oil. Place the chicken in a single layer into the air fryer basket. Do not overcrowd the air fryer basket or place chicken on top of each other.
  4. Cook at 380°F for 10 minutes. Then, flip the chicken and cook for another 7-10 minutes, until no longer pink and an instant thermometer reads 165°F. Depending on the size of your air fryer, you may need to work in batches so that the wings are not overcrowded.
  5. Transfer the chicken wings to a plate and serve with your favorite dipping sauce. Enjoy!
  6. How To Make Crispy Oven Baked Wings
  7. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  8. Season the wings with the seasonings and baking powder.
  9. Place the seasoned wings on an oven-safe wire rack in a single layer. Bake for 45-1 hour, flipping every 20 minutes. The total cook time will depend on the size of the wings but may take up to 1 hour. Use an instant thermometer to determine doneness, it should read 165°F when fully cooked.
