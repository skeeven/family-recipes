Orange Chicken

Main DishChicken
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lbs Chicken
  • 4 cups cooked rice w/ bone broth
  • ⅓ cup corn starch into Chicken
  • 1½ tsp honey
  • 1 cup + 1½ tbsp light Orange Juice
  • 3 tbsp + 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 2½ tbsp corn starch into sauce

Directions

  1. cube Chicken
  2. Season w/ Salt, Pepper & paprika
  3. Mix in cornstarch
  4. Pan Fry 4-5 min each side
  5. whilst cooking blend sauce up
  6. Blend OJ, soy Sauce, honey, garlic powder & corn starch
  7. Pour into Pan until thick (low heat)
  8. Add Chicken
