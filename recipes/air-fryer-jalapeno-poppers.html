Air Fryer Jalapeno Poppers

Air Fryer
Servings12
PrepPT10M
CookPT5M

Ingredients

  • 6 jalapeño peppers
  • 6 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 3/4 cup shredded cheese (I used colby jack)
  • 4 slices bacon, cooked & crumbled
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon panko bread crumbs

Directions

  1. Cut the jalapeños in half lengthwise and remove the seeds and membranes.
  2. In a small mixing bowl, combine the cream cheese and garlic salt and mix well. Stir in 1/2 cup of shredded cheese.
  3. Divide the cream cheese mixture evenly among the pepper halves. Sprinkle additional cheese on top. Top with crumbled bacon and panko bread crumbs.
  4. Place the poppers in the air fryer basket and air fry at 350° F for 5 minutes, until cheese is melted. Top with sliced green onions and serve with ranch dressing.
