McDonald's Breakfast Sauce Recipe

SaucesSauce
Servings1 cup
PrepPT5M
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons mustard
  • 1 tablespoon steak sauce
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon dried dill
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Place mayonnaise, mustard, steak sauce, lemon juice, dill, salt, and pepper in a small mixing bow.
  2. Stir with a spoon, fork, or whisk until well combined.
  3. Serve immediately or store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
