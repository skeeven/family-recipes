Bacon, Egg, and Cheese Crescent Roll Carrot

BreakfastEgg
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • * 1 can (8 oz) refrigerated crescent roll dough
  • * 6 large eggs
  • * ¼ cup milk
  • * 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • * 1 cup Kraft shredded cheddar cheese
  • * ½ tsp salt
  • * ¼ tsp black pepper
  • * ½ tsp garlic powder
  • * 1 tbsp butter
  • * 1 egg
  • * 1 tbsp water
  • * Fresh parsley sprigs

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F line baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. whisk together eggs, milk, salt, pepper, garlic powder.
  3. Scramble eggs in skillet over medium heat
  4. Unroll crescent dough and arrange triangles into a large carrot shape, press seams together.
  5. Spread scrambled eggs over dough, top with bacon and shredded cheddar cheese.
  6. Fold dough edges over filling, sealing the crescent.
  7. Beat 1 egg with 1 tbsp water. Brush over dough
  8. Bake for 18-22 mins, or until golden
  9. Let cool slightly and garnish with fresh parsley sprigs.
  10. Slice and serve warm.
