Air Fryer Beef Kabobs

Air FryerBeef
Servings4 servings
PrepPT30M
CookPT8M

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds sirloin steak (cut into 1-inch chunks)
  • 1 large bell pepper (color of choice)
  • 1 large red onion (or onion of choice)
  • 4 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • salt and pepper (pinch)

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the beef and ingredients for the marinade until fully combined. Cover and marinate in the fridge for 30 minutes or up to 24 hours.
  2. When ready to cook, preheat the air fryer to 400F. Thread the beef, pepper, and onion onto skewers.
  3. Place skewers into the preheated air fryer and air fryer 8-10 minutes, turning halfway through until charred on the outside and tender on the inside.
