Lean Beef Burger

Main DishBeef
Servings1
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 168 grams (3/4 cup) 96/4 lean ground beef
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 1 Aldi brioche bun
  • 3 slices fully cooked bacon
  • 2 slices Velveeta cheese
  • 8 grams (~1/2) tbsp Hellmann's light mayo
  • 8 grams (1/2 tbsp) sugar free ketchup

Directions

  1. Split 168 grams (3/4 cup) 96/4 lean ground beef into two balls and add salt and pepper to them. Form each ball into a square (Wendy's forms their patties into squares). Place the patties in a pan over medium heat, flipping them until they are brown on both sides.
  2. Remove the patties from the pan and add a bun to the same pan to toast.
  3. While the buns are toasting, microwave 3 slices of bacon for 15-17 seconds on a paper towel.
  4. Assemble your burger by adding 1 patty to the bottom bun, 1 slice Velveeta cheese, 1 1/2 slices bacon, the 2nd patty, another Velveeta cheese slice and 1 1/2 slices bacon. Spread 8 grams (~1/2) tbsp light mayo on the top bun and 8 grams (1/2 tbsp) sugar free ketchup. Add the top bun to the burger.
