Cheesy Ranch Ground Beef and Rice Casserole

Main DishBeef
Servings6
PrepPT10M
CookPT35M

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon Olive Oil
  • 1 ½ pounds Ground Beef
  • 1 (1-ounce) Hidden Valley® Original Ranch® Seasoning, Salad Dressing & Recipe Mix Packet
  • 2 cups Cooked Long Grain Rice
  • 1 cup Fresh Or Frozen Peas
  • 1 ½ cups Chicken Broth
  • 2 cups Cheddar Cheese, Shredded

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, warm the olive oil. Add the ground beef and cook, stirring, until browned, about 6–8 minutes. Drain and return to the skillet.
  2. Turn the heat down to medium and add the seasoning packet. Stir in the rice, peas, broth, and 1 cup of cheese. Let simmer for 3 minutes.
  3. Transfer everything to a 9×13 baking dish and bake for 15 minutes. Remove from oven and top with remaining cheese. Return to oven and bake for an additional 10 minutes. Serve hot.
