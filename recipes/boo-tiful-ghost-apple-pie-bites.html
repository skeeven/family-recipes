Boo-tiful Ghost Apple Pie Bites

Dessert
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 2 pie crusts
  • 2 cups chopped apples
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ½–1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ⅛ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp butter, cubed (optional)
  • Coarse sugar for sprinkling (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix apples, sugar, flour, vanilla, cinnamon, and nutmeg in a bowl.
  3. Roll out pie crusts and cut ghost shapes using a cookie cutter. Place half on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  4. Spoon filling onto each, adding butter if desired.
  5. Top with remaining ghost shapes, sealing edges and crimping with a fork.
  6. Brush with egg wash (egg mixed with milk) and sprinkle with coarse sugar.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden brown. Cool slightly, then enjoy!
