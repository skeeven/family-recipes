Baked Stuffed Shells

PrepPT0S
CookPT30M

Ingredients

  • 1 box jumbo pasta shells
  • 1 lb ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 1/2 cups Lifeway Farmer Cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • Salt + pepper, to taste
  • Fresh basil, for garnish

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook pasta shells until al dente.
  3. Drain and set aside.
  4. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the ground meat until browned.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of Italian seasoning.
  6. In a large bowl, mix together the farmer cheese, egg, mozzarella, parmesan, garlic powder, and cooked meat.
  7. Spread a layer of marinara sauce on the bottom of a 9x13" baking dish.
  8. Fill each shell with cheese and meat mixture, then place in the baking dish.
  9. Top with remaining marinara sauce & sprinkle of mozzarella.
  10. Bake uncovered for 25-30 mins.
  11. Garnish with fresh basil and serve warm.
