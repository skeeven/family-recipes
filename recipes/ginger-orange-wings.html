Ginger-Orange Wings

AppetizerChicken
Servings50 pieces
PrepPT45M
CookPT10M

Ingredients

  • 25 whole chicken wings (about 5 pounds)
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 teaspoons seasoned salt
  • 2 teaspoons garlic salt
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups orange marmalade
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 3/4 teaspoon ground ginger

Directions

  1. Cut chicken wings into 3 sections; discard wing tip section.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the flour, seasoned salt and garlic salt.
  3. Add chicken wings, in batches, and toss to coat.
  4. In a large cast-iron or other heavy skillet, fry wings in oil, in batches, until golden and crispy, 3-4 minutes.
  5. Drain pan drippings;
  6. return all chicken to the skillet.
  7. Combine the marmalade, ketchup, soy sauce and ginger;
  8. pour over chicken and stir to coat.
  9. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 10-15 minutes.
