Chicken Enchilada

CrockpotChicken
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1/2 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 16 oz jarred enchilada sauce
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 4 corn (or flour) tortillas, cut into strips
  • 15 oz can low-sodium black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 4 oz Mexican blend shredded cheese
  • sliced jalapeño peppers, and chopped fresh cilantro, for topping

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a skillet.
  2. Add onion, garlic, cumin, and salt and cook, until softened.
  3. Transfer to the slow cooker, add enchilada sauce, sour cream, 1/4 cup water to mixture and stir.
  4. Fold in the beans add the chicken.
  5. Cover and cook on high for about 3 to 4 hours or on low for about 6 to 7.
  6. Shred the chicken in the pot with 2 forks.
  7. Add tortilla strips, stir, then top with the cheese, cover & cook 30 minutes.
  8. Garnish with cilantro, jalapenos, and optional toppings.
