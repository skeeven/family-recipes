Natural Leavening Waffles

BreakfastBread
Servings4 people
PrepPT15M
CookPT3M

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ cup coconut oil
  • 2 Tbsp sweetener (maple syrup, honey, agave)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 Tbsp vanilla
  • 1 dash cinnamon
  • 2 cups natural leavening -- sourdough starter ((discard that is old works GREAT!))
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tbsp water

Directions

  1. Mix eggs, coconut oil, sweetener, salt, vanilla and cinnamon all together
  2. Add natural leavening and mix well.
  3. Mix baking soda and water, then add to batter
  4. Stir quickly, it will raise in volume and get light and bubbly
  5. Bake on waffle iron
