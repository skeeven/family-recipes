Shamrock Pudding Cups Recipe

Servings6–8 cups
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • Pudding Layer
  • 1 box (3.4 oz) instant vanilla pudding mix
  • 2 cups cold milk
  • 1/2 tsp mint extract (or peppermint extract)
  • 3–5 drops green food coloring
  • Cookie Layers
  • 16–20 Oreo cookies, crushed
  • Topping
  • 1–2 cups whipped cream or Cool Whip
  • Green sanding sugar or sprinkles
  • Optional Upgrades (make them even better)
  • Shamrock Shake flavor
  • Replace vanilla pudding with white chocolate pudding
  • Add 1 tbsp cream cheese when mixing.
  • Extra crunch
  • Add thin mint cookies or mint Oreos instead of regular Oreos.
  • Fancy presentation
  • Top with:
  • mini chocolate chips
  • Andes mint pieces
  • a small chocolate shamrock.

Directions

  1. Make the pudding
  2. In a bowl, whisk together:
  3. pudding mix
  4. cold milk
  5. Mix for about 2 minutes until thick.
  6. Stir in:
  7. mint extract
  8. green food coloring
  9. Chill 5–10 minutes so it firms up.
  10. Crush the Oreos
  11. Place Oreos in a zip bag.
  12. Crush with a rolling pin until they look like crumbly dirt.
  13. You can include the filling—it helps them stick together.
  14. Layer the cups
  15. In small clear cups:
  16. Oreo crumbs (bottom layer)
  17. Green mint pudding
  18. Another Oreo crumb layer
  19. Repeat if the cup is tall.
  20. Add the topping
  21. Pipe or spoon whipped cream on top.
  22. Sprinkle with green sanding sugar.
