Haitian Rice and Beans (Diri ak Pwa)

Side Dish
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 3 cups rice
  • 1 cup dry beans (Pinto, red, or black beans)
  • 8 cups water
  • 3 tbs olive oil
  • 1 tbs butter
  • 1 large onion (diced)
  • 1 tsp salt (or to taste)
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 3 cloves (1/4 tsp ground cloves)
  • 3 cubes chicken bouillon
  • 1 whole scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 1/2 tsp thyme (2 sprigs)
  • 1 cup Coconut Milk

Directions

  1. Step 1
  2. In a large pot, add the water, beans, salt, 1 tbs olive oil and allow the beans to cook for about 1 hour on medium high heat or until beans are soft.
  3. Step 2
  4. Once cooked, strain the water into a seperate container for later use.
  5. Step 3
  6. In another large pot, add 1 tbs of olive oil, and sauté the onions and garlic for about 2 minutes.
  7. Step 4
  8. Add coconut milk, chicken bouillon, beans and mix thoroughly.
  9. Step 5
  10. Add cloves and 6 cups of the bean water to the pot and bring to boil.
  11. Step 6
  12. Mix in the rice and whole scotch bonnet pepper and allow the rice to cook for about 20 min or until there is just a little amount of water left.
  13. Step 7
  14. Reduce the heat to medium,add thyme, butter, and cover the pot with a lid and allow the remaining water to be absorbed. About 10-15 min.
  15. Step 8
  16. Once cooked fluff the rice and serve.
