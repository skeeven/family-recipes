The Best Easy Air Fryer Pizza Roll Ups

Air Fryer
Servings5 Pieces
PrepPT5M
CookPT8M

Ingredients

  • 5 Small Tortillas (8"-10" size. If smaller no need to fold in the sides)
  • 25 slices Pepperoni
  • 2 cups Shredded Mozzarella Cheese (or 5 String Cheese)
  • 1/3 cup Pizza Sauce
  • 2 tbsp Shredded Parmesan Cheese (optional)
  • 1 tbsp Butter (melted)
  • 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Italian Seasoning
  • 1/4 tsp Garlic Salt

Directions

  1. Assemble Pizza Rolls
  2. Lay your tortilla onto a plate, and spread a heaping spoonful of tomato sauce, top with cheese and lay 5 pepperoni across.
  3. Fold the 2 sides in and roll up the tortilla. Set the Pizza Roll into the Air Fryer seam side down.
  4. Repeat until all the rolls are ready.
  5. Butter Topping
  6. Heat butter in a dish for 30-45 seconds until melted. Add in the seasonings and mix together:
  7. Brush the butter mixture over the pizza rolls and top with shredded parmesan.
  8. Air fry at 400 for 8-9 minutes or bake at 400 for 12-15 minutes
