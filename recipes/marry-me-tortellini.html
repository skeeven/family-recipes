Marry Me Tortellini

Main DishPasta
PrepPT20M
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Pinch red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 20 oz cheese tortellini
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1 cup grated parmesan

Directions

  1. Sauté garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, seasoning & flakes in olive oil.
  2. Stir in flour & cook 2-3 min.
  3. Add broth & bring to boil.
  4. Add tortellini & simmer 5-7 min until tender.
  5. Remove from heat, stir in cream, parmesan, & spinach.
  6. Season to taste.
