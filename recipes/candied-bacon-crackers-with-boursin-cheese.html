Candied Bacon Crackers with Boursin Cheese

Appetizer
PrepPT0S
CookPT40M

Ingredients

  • 1 package Club crackers
  • 8 oz Boursin cheese
  • 6 slices bacon
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1-2 tsp Dan-O's Cheesoning
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil and place a wire rack on top.
  2. Cut bacon into 4ths (the length of cracker).
  3. Arrange Club crackers on the baking sheet. Spread Boursin cheese on each cracker.
  4. Top with bacon, then sprinkle with brown sugar and Dan-O's seasoning and red pepper flakes.
  5. Bake for 40 mins, until bacon is caramelized.
  6. Cool slightly, then serve warm or at room temperature. Enjoy!
