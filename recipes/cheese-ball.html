Cheese Ball

Appetizer
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • Ingredients
  • 12 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Coating
  • 1 cup pecans, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. To your stand mixer add the cream cheese, mayonnaise, parmesan cheese, garlic powder, oregano and salt until smooth, about 1-2 minutes.
  2. Cut a large piece of plastic wrap and add the cheese mixture to the middle.
  3. Wrap into a ball and refrigerate for 1 hour.
  4. Roll the cheese ball into the chopped pecans and serve chilled.
  5. For the turkey - I used pretzel sticks for feathers, turkey beef stick for the neck. For the face use a round cracker and use icing to add candy eyes and candy corn beak
