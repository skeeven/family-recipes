Chick-fil-A Hash Brown Scramble Burrito Dupe

BreakfastBreakfast
Servings6
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • What You Need
  • 6 Mission Carb Balance tortillas
  • 230 grams (~1 cup) egg beaters
  • 98 grams (~1 cup) Kraft fat free shredded cheddar cheese
  • 18 pieces Ore-Ida golden crispy potato crowns
  • 224 grams (1 cup) Realgood lightly breaded chicken nuggets

Directions

  1. Air fry 224 grams (1 cup) chicken nuggets at 330 degrees for 20 minutes.
  2. Air fry 18 potato crowns at 400 degrees for 10-12 minutes (shake basket after 6 minutes).
  3. Rinse 98 grams (~1 cup) fat free shredded cheddar cheese.
  4. Spray a pan with cooking spray and cook 230 grams (~1 cup) egg beaters over medium heat.
  5. Add in 56 grams (~1 cup) fat free shredded cheddar cheese to the egg mixture and incorporate into the eggs.
  6. Chop your nuggets up into tiny pieces.
  7. To make the burrito, add 3 hash browns, on the egg mixture, 1/6 of the chopped nuggets, and grams of rinsed fat free shredded cheddar cheese (1/6 of the cheese you have left over after you added the 56 grams to the egg mixture).
  8. Fold into burritos and store in the fridge for 4-5 days or they can freeze for quite a while.
  9. If you eat the burrito right away, I suggest spraying a pan with cooking spray and brown the burrito on each side in the pan.
  10. You can also optionally serve your burrito with a side of salsa for some sauce.
