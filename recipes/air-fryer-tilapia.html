Air Fryer Tilapia

Air FryerFish
Servings4 tilapia
PrepPT5M
CookPT10M

Ingredients

  • 1 pound tilapia fillets
  • salt and pepper
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 1 Tablespoon olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves minced
  • 1 Tablespoon Cajun Seasoning

Directions

  1. Salt and Pepper the tilapia fillets.
  2. To make the Cajun Garlic Butter Sauce
  3. In a small bowl whisk together the melted butter, olive oil, garlic and cajun seasoning. Spread evenly over the fish.
  4. Place the fish in the basket of the air fryer. Cook at 290 degrees for 5 minutes. Flip the fish and cook for 3-4 minutes more or until flaky and opaque.
