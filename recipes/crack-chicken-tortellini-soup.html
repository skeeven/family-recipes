Crack chicken tortellini soup

CrockpotSoup
PrepPT0S
CookPT6H

Ingredients

  • 2-3 chicken breasts
  • 1 packet ranch seasoning
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 (8 oz) block cream cheese
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup cooked & crumbled bacon
  • 1 (20 oz) package refrigerated cheese tortellini
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Optional: chopped spinach or green onions for garnish

Directions

  1. Add chicken, ranch seasoning, broth, cream cheese, and cheddar to the crockpot.
  2. Cover and cook on low for 6-7 hours or high for 3-4 hours.
  3. Shred the chicken in the pot once cooked.
  4. Stir in bacon, heavy cream, and tortellini.
  5. Cook on high for another 15-20 minutes until tortellini is tender.
  6. Garnish and serve!
