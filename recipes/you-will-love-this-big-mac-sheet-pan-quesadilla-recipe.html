You Will Love This Big Mac Sheet Pan Quesadilla Recipe!

Main DishBeef
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 2 lbs lean ground beef
  • 15 slices American cheese
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup onion (diced)
  • Dill pickle chips
  • ½ cup mayonnaise (you can also use light mayo)
  • 1 Tbsp ketchup
  • 1 Tbsp mustard
  • 1 Tbsp pickle relish
  • 2 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Spray pan with cooking spray.
  2. Cook ground beef and set aside.
  3. Place 6 tortillas around the edges of the pan. Lay American cheese over the tortillas, next spread the meat mixture. Add pickles and diced onion.
  4. In a small bowl, combine sauce ingredients and drizzle over the quesadilla. Sprinkle cheddar cheese on top of the sauce mixture.
  5. Place the last two tortilla in the center and fold the tortillas towards the middle.
  6. Place the bottom of a sheet pan on top (to hold tortillas in place) and bake for 20 mins.
  7. Cool slightly before cutting and serve with sauce for dipping.
