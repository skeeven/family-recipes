High-Protein Broccoli Cheddar Chicken & Rice

Main DishChicken
Servings8
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • 3 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • Chicken Seasoning
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper, optional
  • Vegetables
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 lb 2 oz broccoli florets, chopped (about 5–6 cups)
  • Rice
  • 3 cups dry basmati rice
  • Sauce
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 cups freshly shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup freshly shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard, optional but recommended

Directions

  1. Cook the rice:
  2. Cook the 3 cups basmati rice separately according to the package or rice-cooker instructions.
  3. Season the chicken:
  4. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces. Combine the salt, pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, and optional cayenne. Toss with the chicken until evenly coated.
  5. Cook the chicken:
  6. Heat a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken until browned and cooked through to 165°F. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  7. Cook the vegetables:
  8. Reduce heat to medium and add the butter. Add the diced onion and carrots and cook until they begin to soften, about 5–7 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.
  9. Add the broccoli:
  10. Add the chopped broccoli and chicken broth. Cover and simmer for several minutes until the broccoli is tender but still bright green.
  11. Make the cheese sauce:
  12. Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the milk and Dijon mustard. Gradually add the cheddar, mozzarella, and Parmesan, stirring until melted and creamy. Avoid boiling the sauce after adding the cheese.
  13. Combine:
  14. Return the cooked chicken to the pan and stir until coated in the broccoli-cheddar sauce.
  15. Add the rice:
  16. Stir the cooked rice into the chicken mixture, or divide the rice among 8 containers and spoon the chicken and broccoli mixture over the top.
  17. Meal prep:
  18. Divide evenly into 8 servings. Refrigerate leftovers in airtight containers.
