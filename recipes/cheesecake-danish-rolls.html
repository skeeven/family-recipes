Cheesecake Danish Rolls

DessertBread
PrepPT0S
CookPT25M

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon butter, melted
  • 1 package (12 rolls) King's Hawaiian sweet rolls
  • 1/3 cup butter, melted (for filling)
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1/2 tablespoon ground cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg (optional)
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar

Directions

  1. Preheat Oven: Preheat to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Prepare Filling: Beat cream cheese with granulated sugar and beaten egg until smooth.
  3. Prep Rolls: Place rolls in a greased baking dish and cut a pocket in each.
  4. Cinnamon Mixture: Mix melted butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
  5. Fill Rolls: Spoon cream cheese filling into each roll. Drizzle with cinnamon mixture.
  6. Bake: Bake for 20-25 minutes, until golden and set.
  7. Serve: Let cool slightly and enjoy
