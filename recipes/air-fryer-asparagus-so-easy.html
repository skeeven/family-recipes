Air Fryer Asparagus (So Easy)

Air FryerVegetable
Servings4 servings
PrepPT10M
CookPT7M

Ingredients

  • 2 lb asparagus
  • 1 tbsp garlic parsley salt ((adjsut to taste))
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/3 cup parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Cut off the ends of asparagus. Line them up in one layer inside your air fryer.
  2. Sprinkle them with oil, garlic parsley salt and parmesan.
  3. Air fry asparagus at 400F for 7 minutes or until they are golden brown. Optional sprinkle asparagus with a tiny bit more of parmesan.
