Seven-Layer Bean Dip

Appetizer
Servings8
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 1 can refried beans (large)
  • 3 avocados
  • 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
  • garlic salt (to taste)
  • 1 8-oz. container sour cream
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 tsp. pepper
  • 1/2 tsp. taco seasoning
  • hot pepper sauce (to taste)
  • 1 bunch green onions
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 can pitted olives (chopped)
  • 8 oz. shredded Cheddar cheese (or taco cheese)

Directions

  1. On a plate, layer refried beans.
  2. Mash avocados and mix with lemon juice and garlic salt.
  3. Mix sour cream and mayonnaise together with salt, pepper, taco seasoning, and hot pepper sauce.
  4. Layer avocado mix on top of refried beans.
  5. Layer sour cream-mayonnaise mix on top of avocado mix.
  6. Top, in turn, with green onions, tomatoes, olives, and cheese.
  7. Serve with tortilla or pita chips.
