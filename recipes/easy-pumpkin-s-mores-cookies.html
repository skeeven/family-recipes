Easy Pumpkin S'mores Cookies

DessertCookie
PrepPT0S
CookPT12M

Ingredients

  • 1 package refrigerated pumpkin cookie dough (or seasonal pumpkin spice dough)
  • Graham crackers, broken into squares
  • Hershey's chocolate bars, broken into squares
  • Large marshmallows, cut in half

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Place a graham cracker square on the baking sheet. Top with a piece of Hershey's chocolate, then half of a marshmallow.
  3. Take a scoop of pumpkin cookie dough and flatten it slightly with your hands. Place over the marshmallow, pinching edges to seal as best you can.
  4. Bake 10-12 minutes, until cookies are golden and set around the edges.
  5. Let cool for a few minutes before enjoying the gooey s'mores center!
