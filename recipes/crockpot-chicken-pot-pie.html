Crockpot Chicken Pot Pie

Main DishChicken
PrepPT0S
CookPT6H

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 cans Heinz Home Style Herbed Gravy (15.5 oz each)
  • 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables (16 oz)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 Yukon potatoes, diced
  • 1 cup celery, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 can refrigerated crescent roll dough (8 oz)
  • 2 cups chicken broth

Directions

  1. Place chicken in crockpot.
  2. Add vegetables, onion, potatoes, celery, garlic, broth, and gravy.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Cook on low for 6-7 hours.
  5. Shred chicken and stir.
  6. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  7. Roll out crescent dough on a baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes, until golden.
  8. Serve the pot pie filling with baked crescent rolls on top.
  9. Enjoy!
