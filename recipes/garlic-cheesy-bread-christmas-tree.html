Garlic Cheesy Bread Christmas Tree

Side DishBread
Servings40 mini
PrepPT0S
CookPT15M

Ingredients

  • 12 King's Hawaiian Sweet Rolls
  • 3-4 mozzarella cheese sticks
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Grated Parmesan (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Arrange rolls in a tree shape on a baking sheet.
  2. Cut mozzarella sticks into pieces and insert into rolls.
  3. Mix melted butter, garlic powder, parsley, basil, salt, and pepper. Brush over rolls.
  4. Bake for 12-15 minutes until golden and cheese melts.
  5. Optionally, sprinkle Parmesan on top while warm. Serve and enjoy!
