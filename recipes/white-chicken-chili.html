White Chicken Chili

CrockpotChicken
PrepPT0S
CookPT4H

Ingredients

  • 2 Chicken Breasts
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Pepper
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • ½ tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 2 cans Northern beans
  • 1 can Whole corn
  • 1 can Diced tomatoes with rotel
  • 1Med onion
  • 32 oz Chicken broth
  • ¼ cup Heavy cream
  • 8oz Cream cheese

Directions

  1. Add into crock pot and cook on High for 4-8 hours.
  2. After hours, pull chicken with fork and add back to soup.
  3. Add 1/4 cup heavy cream and 8 ounces cream cheese cut up.
  4. Cover for 20-30 minutes.
  5. Serve with chips and shredded cheese.
