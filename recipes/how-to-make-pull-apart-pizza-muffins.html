How to Make Pull-Apart Pizza Muffins!

AppetizerBread
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 16 oz tube of jumbo biscuits (regular NOT flaky)
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup chopped sliced pepperoni
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon parsley flakes
  • 1 cup of marinara for serving

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees.
  2. Spray a 12-cup muffin pan with cooking spray and set aside. Remove biscuits from tube and cut into roughly 6 pieces.
  3. Place chopped biscuits in a large bowl along with remaining ingredients and gently mix together. Scoop mixture evenly and place into prepared muffin tin.
  4. Bake on center rack in preheated oven for 12-15 minutes until golden brown.
  5. Cool for a few minutes then run a knife around the edge of the muffins to pop out. Serve with a side of warm marinara for dunking if desired.
