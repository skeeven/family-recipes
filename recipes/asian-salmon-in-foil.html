Asian Salmon in Foil

Main DishFish
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds salmon
  • 1 teaspoon garlic
  • 1 tablespoon ginger
  • 2 green onions
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sriracha
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients except for green onions in a bowl. Place salmon on foil in a shallow pan. Pour sauce over salmon and fold the foil around the salmon.
  2. Bake at 375° for 40 minutes.
  3. Open foil and add green onions.
  4. Leave foil open and put in broiler for 3-5 minutes.
  5. Serve over rice.
