Beef Taco Pockets

Main DishBeef
Servings8
PrepPT10M
CookPT30M

Ingredients

  • 12-6 inch flour tortillas
  • 1 ½ cups Cheddar Cheese, shredded
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 8 ounces Cream Cheese, softened to room temperature
  • ½ cup Salsa
  • 1 pound Ground beef, lean
  • 1 envelope Taco Seasoning mix
  • Sour Cream for serving

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350*
  2. Grease a baking tray or line with a sheet of parchment paper
  3. In a skillet, crumble and brown the ground beef
  4. Drain the fat from the skillet when done cooking
  5. Add in the packet of Taco Seasoning Mix, follow the packet instructions for the amount of water to use
  6. In a bowl, beat the cream cheese until it is smooth, add in the ½ cup salsa and blend well
  7. Lay the tortillas on a flat surface in front of you
  8. Spoon a good amount of the cream cheese salsa combination on the center of each tortilla
  9. Place a spoonful of the ground beef over the cream cheese mix
  10. Sprinkle with some of the shredded cheese
  11. Continue this for each of the tortillas
  12. Now you need to roll each tortilla as though you are rolling a burrito
  13. Place each ‘pocket’ on the lined baking tray
  14. Brush with melted butter and add a tiny bit more of shredded cheese.
  15. Bake for about 15 minutes
  16. Serve with your favorite sauces
