Easy Slow Cooker Recipe - French Dip with Au Jus

Servings4-6
PrepPT0S
CookPT9H

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs. beef chuck roast
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp dried parsley
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 2 tsp dried dill
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • For Servings
  • 4-6 Ciabatta Rolls

Directions

  1. Dump all ingredients, except for serving, into the slow cooker.
  2. Cook on low 9-10 hours.
  3. Remove beef, shred, and serve on bread with au jus sauce from slow cooker on the side for dipping.
