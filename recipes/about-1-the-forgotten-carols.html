About 1 — The Forgotten Carols

PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 1 doz. boiled eggs (mashed with a potato masher)
  • Radishes, bacon bits, green onions, celery (to taste)
  • 1 c. mayonnaise
  • 1 T. mustard
  • 1/2-1 c. cool whip
  • 1 t. salt
  • 1 t. ground pepper
  • 1 t. curry

Directions

  1. Mix together the mayo, mustard, cool whip, salt, pepper and curry then combine it with the mashed egg mixture.
