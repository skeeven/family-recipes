Garlic Butter Chicken Alfredo Pasta with Broccoli

CrockpotChicken
Servings8
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • 5 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp garlic paste
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Pasta & Sauce
  • 2 jars Bettergoods Alfredo Sauce
  • 2 boxes protein pasta, such as rotini
  • 4 cups broccoli florets, steamed

Directions

  1. Season the chicken: Place the chicken breasts in a slow cooker. Add the garlic paste, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, water, and butter.
  2. Cook the chicken: Cover and cook on LOW for 6–7 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours, until the chicken is tender and reaches an internal temperature of at least 165°F.
  3. Shred the chicken: Shred the cooked chicken directly in the slow cooker using two forks. Stir it into the cooking juices.
  4. Cook the pasta: Prepare the protein pasta according to the package directions. Drain well.
  5. Steam the broccoli: Steam the broccoli until tender-crisp. Avoid overcooking it since it will soften further when mixed with the hot sauce.
  6. Add the Alfredo: Pour both jars of Alfredo sauce into the slow cooker with the shredded chicken. Stir well and allow it to warm through.
  7. Combine: Add the cooked pasta and steamed broccoli. Gently stir until the pasta, chicken, and broccoli are evenly coated in Alfredo sauce.
  8. Serve or meal prep: Divide into 8 equal portions.
