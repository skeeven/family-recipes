Air Fryer Frozen Chicken Cordon Bleu

Air FryerChicken
Servings2
PrepPT0S
CookPT18M

Ingredients

  • 2 frozen chicken cordon bleu

Directions

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 380 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Once the air fryer is hot, add the frozen chicken cordon bleu.
  3. Cook the frozen chicken cordon bleu for 17-20 minutes total until fully cooked. Flip the pieces halfway through the cooking time. The internal temperature of the chicken should be 165 degrees Fahrenheit. The cheese might start to ooze out of the breading towards the end of the cooking time.
  4. When cooked through, remove the cordon bleu and let them sit for 2 minutes before serving. They taste great with a side of potatoes and vegetables.
