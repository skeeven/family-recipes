Creamy Pasta e Fagioli

CrockpotPasta
PrepPT0S
CookPT4H

Ingredients

  • 2 TBSP Olive Oil
  • 1 small onion diced
  • 3 garlic cloves minced
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • 1-2 cans cannellini beans drained & rinsed
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 TSP Salt
  • 1/2 TSP Pepper
  • 1/2 TSP Italian Seasoning
  • 1 cup uncooked ditalini pasta
  • 1 Boursin Cheese round
  • Shredded parmesan & mozzarella cheese
  • Optional 4 chopped Tomatoes

Directions

  1. Add everything but the Boursin cheese to the crock pot and stir.
  2. Add in the cheese and cover.
  3. Cook on high 4 hours or low 8 hours.
  4. Stir to combine Boursin cheese and serve with parmesan & mozzarella cheeses.
