Crockpot BBQ Chicken & Beans

CrockpotChicken
Servings4 servings
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breast
  • ¾ cup low-sugar BBQ sauce
  • 3 tbsp chicken broth
  • ½ tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • ½ tbsp Worcestershire
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ¾ tsp onion powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¾ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp chili powder
  • ¼ tsp cayenne (optional)
  • ½ onion
  • ¾ bell pepper
  • ½ tomato
  • 8 oz black beans
  • ½ cup corn

Directions

  1. Put all ingredients in a ziplock bag
  2. Mix it marinate over night
  3. Empty into crockpot
  4. Cook on high for 3-4 hours or high 6-7 hours
  5. Shred chicken
  6. Serve with carb or veggie of your choice
