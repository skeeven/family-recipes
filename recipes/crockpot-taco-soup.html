Crockpot Taco Soup

CrockpotSoup
PrepPT0S
CookPT6H

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (15.5 oz) can pinto beans, drained & rinsed
  • 2 (10 oz) cans diced tomatoes with chilies, with juices
  • 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • 2 bell peppers, diced
  • 1 (4 oz) can mild green chiles, with juices
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded & diced
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 tsp chili powder

Directions

  1. Brown beef in a skillet, drain excess grease. Add onion & garlic, cook 2-3 min.
  2. Transfer to crockpot. Add all remaining ingredients & stir.
  3. Cook on low 6-8 hrs or high 3-4 hrs.
  4. Adjust seasoning & serve with toppings like cheese, sour cream, or tortilla chips. Enjoy!
