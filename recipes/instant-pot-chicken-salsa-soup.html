Instant Pot Chicken Salsa Soup

Insta potSoup
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 3 cups low sodium chicken broth
  • 1 (8 oz ) brick cream cheese
  • 1 ½ cups mild salsa
  • 4 tsp taco seasoning mix
  • Toppings
  • 1 avocado, thinly sliced or diced
  • ½ cup Shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Directions

  1. Soften cream cheese:
  2. Cut cream cheese into a cubes. Set aside to soft and while you work on the next step.
  3. Pressure Cook:
  4. Stir together chicken broth salsa and taco seasoning and pressure cooker pot until well mixed. Somers chicken breast in mixture. Secure insulin. Cook for 25 minutes at high pressure, followed by quick pressure release.
  5. Thicken Soup:
  6. Uncover and transfer only chicken breast to plate; set aside. Whisk together one cup of hot liquid from pressure cooker with softened cream cheese in heat proof bowl or liquid measuring glass until smooth, a few minutes. Pour cream cheese mixture into pot, stirring it in.
  7. Shred Chicken:
  8. Turn on sauté mode for medium heat. While waiting for liquid to boil, finely shred chicken using two forks and return chicken to pot. Boil soup for a few minutes to combine flavors, stirring frequently, then turn off sauté mode.
  9. Serve:
  10. Ladle soup into bowls, and top with avocado, cheddar cheese, and parsley.
