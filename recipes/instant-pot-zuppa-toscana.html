Instant Pot Zuppa Toscana

Insta potSoup
Servings12
PrepPT2M
CookPT20M

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
  • 24 ounces No-Sugar All Natural Pork Sausage (2 - 12 ounce packages )
  • 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 6 cups chicken stock
  • 6 medium russet potatoes (6 - 8, cut into 1/2" cubes)
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 5 ounces baby kale

Directions

  1. Turn the 6-quart Instant Pot to "Saute". Once you can feel the heat when you hold your hand above it, add olive oil and crumble in the sausage.
  2. Cook, stirring occasionally and breaking up the sausage as desired (I like bigger chunks), until the sausage is cooked through, about 3-5 minutes. Drain excess the sausage drippings from the pan (if necessary).
  3. Add the Italian seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder, red pepper flakes and salt. Stir to combine.
  4. Add the potatoes and pour in the chicken stock. Stir to combine and scrape the bits from the bottom of the pot.
  5. Close the lid on the Instant Pot. Select "Manual" setting on the Instant Pot. Turn the pressure to high. Set timer for 8 minutes. Make sure the vent is closed.
  6. When cook time is complete, quick-release pressure according to manufacturer's directions.
  7. Use a slotted spoon to remove 2 cups of potatoes from the pot and place them in a bowl. Mash the potatoes and pour them back into the pot; this will help thicken the soup.
  8. Add the cream and kale to the soup, and stir to combine. Continue cooking the soup until the kale is wilted and tender, about 3 more minutes.
  9. Serve and enjoy!
