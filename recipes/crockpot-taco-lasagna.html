Crockpot Taco Lasagna

Main DishCasserole
Servings6-8
PrepPT0S
CookPT4H

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • 3 tablespoons taco seasoning
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 15-ounce can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 15-ounce can red enchilada sauce
  • 2-3 cups shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese
  • 4 large flour tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown beef in a pan, drain, and mix with salsa and 2 tbsp taco seasoning. Add salt and pepper.
  2. Blend cream cheese with 1 tbsp taco seasoning, then mix in black beans.
  3. In a slow cooker, layer 1/3 cup enchilada sauce, a tortilla, 1/3 cup cream cheese mixture, 1 cup taco meat, and 3/4 cup cheese.
  4. Drizzle with 1/3 cup enchilada sauce. Repeat layers 3 times.
  5. Cook on low for 4-5 hours or high for 2-4 hours until bubbly.
  6. Slice into 6-8 pieces and add optional toppings.
