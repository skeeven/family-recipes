EASY TACO CUPS

Main DishBeef
PrepPT0S
CookPT0S

Ingredients

  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 1 lb. ground beef (I used 90/10)
  • ½ of a white onion, diced
  • 14.5 oz. canned petite diced tomatoes (drained)
  • 1/4 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Optional toppings: sour cream, guacamole, chopped cilantro,
  • sliced green onion

Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400 degrees and spray a 12 cup muffin tin with cooking spray.
  2. * heat a large pan over medium high heat and add a splash of olive oil once it's hot. Add your onions, saute until tender.
  3. * add your ground beef and seasonings and cook until the beef is cooked through. Add your canned tomatoes (drained) and continue cooking until almost all of the liquid is absorbed.
  4. * place one wonton wrapper in the center of each cup. Add 1 tablespoon of shredded cheese to each cup and place another wonton over the cheese layer.
  5. * Then fill each cup with your ground beef mixture and sprinkle your remaining cheese over the top.
  6. Place the tin in the oven and bake for 12-15 minutes or until the cheese has melted and the wontons are crispy Serve immediately with your favorite toppings and enjoy!
